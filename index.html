Aliona Munteanu and Dahlia Tremblay, accompanied by her parents, during a private screening of the short film Les Murmures de l’Écho. Together, they had the opportunity to discover the result of Jamie Zampini’s work, who, with the resources available to him, crafted an intriguing film where grief, technology, and imagination intersect.

Jamie Zampini is an independent filmmaker based in Montreal, born in Guatemala and adopted by an Italian-Canadian family.

A graduate of Concordia University’s Film Production program and holder of a College Studies Diploma (AEC) in Graphic Design, he combines storytelling, technical expertise, and self-taught musical composition to create films with a distinctive visual identity and emotional depth.

As a writer and director of short films, three of which have been showcased internationally, he explores identity, memory, grief, and resilience through intimate, deeply human stories.
